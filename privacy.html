Privacy Policy - Kilo

Effective date: March 24, 2026

Kilo ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Kilo mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: We allow login via Google authentication using Supabase.

Images and Media: We collect images that you upload from your camera or photo library, including clothing photos and personal/travel images.

Usage Data: We collect app-related data such as wardrobe items, outfits, schedules, packing lists, and app interactions.

2. Device Permissions

Kilo requires access to your device camera and photo library to allow you to upload images.

You can grant or revoke these permissions at any time through your device settings. If you deny these permissions, some features may not function properly.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

- Provide core app features (wardrobe management, outfit creation, scheduling)

- Improve user experience and app performance

- Fix bugs and provide customer support

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services to operate the app:

- Cloudinary: for image storage and AI background removal

- Supabase: for authentication and database storage

Your data may be processed on external servers as part of these services.

5. Payments and Ads

Kilo offers both free and premium features via in-app purchases through Apple App Store and Google Play.

The app may display advertisements in the free version.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your account and all associated data.

To request deletion, please contact us at the email below. We will process your request within a reasonable timeframe.

Some data may be retained if required by law or for legitimate business purposes.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal data.

9. Children's Privacy

Kilo is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
